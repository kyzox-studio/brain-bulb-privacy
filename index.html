Brain Bulb – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Brain Bulb (the "game", Android package com.kyzox.brainbulb), made by Kyzox ("we", "us"), uses and why.

In short: you do not need an account and we do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells one optional item ("Remove Ads") through Google Play — those Google services handle some data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your own phone, so that you can continue playing:

  • which levels you have unlocked and the stars you earned,
  • your free hints and which answers you have already opened,
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, language),
  • whether you bought "Remove Ads".

We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

2. Internet connection check

The game needs the internet. To check that a connection works, it looks up the addresses of google.com and cloudflare.com every few seconds. No personal information is sent by us in these checks.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a skippable ad after a level is solved, and optional reward videos you can choose to watch (to skip a level, get 60 more seconds or get a hint). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location it reveals,
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (using Google's consent message) before ads may use your data for personalised advertising. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Your payment details are processed by Google; we never see them. The game only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made, so that it can switch off the ads between levels (and restore it if you reinstall). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Children

Brain Bulb is a puzzle game for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone, except that the Google services named above (AdMob and Google Play) process data as described in this policy.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off personalised ads or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the DPDP Act in India or the CCPA in California) you may have rights to access or delete data. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best made to Google; for anything else, contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

9. Contact
